Ten Movies Everyone Should See

Here are ten movies I think everyone should see, separate from my five favorite movies. I've included these movies because they are personal favorites of mine and those around me. They are all highly rated and there is much to take away from each of them. They will leave you thinking about them long after you finish watching.
